The moment we built for

“They say I'm done here Friday. I can't go back under the bridge with this leg. Where do I go?”

Ray, 62, a made-up client from the event's scenarios. Orange County has programs. Finding the one that fits is where people get stuck.

What SAL does

Four simple promises.

☎

Talk, don't type

Call Sal and talk in plain words. English or Spanish.

✓

Only checked facts

Every phone number and hour comes from a list people checked. Claude never writes one.

⌕

Searches while you talk

After every answer, SAL searches 41 programs again and the shortlist narrows.

▤

A packet to take home

Your steps, the places, the numbers and the dates, printed in your language.

How it works

One call. One plan.

Call

The client presses Call. Sal picks up.

Listen

Sal asks a few short questions, one at a time.

Search

The case manager watches the shortlist form, with sources.

Packet

Today's steps, a text draft and a PDF the case manager signs off.

Safe by design

Staff stay in charge.

Safety comes first

If someone is in danger, Sal stops, gives the hotline, and brings in staff. No address is ever shown.

Nothing is sent

Plans and texts are drafts. A case manager reviews before anything reaches the client.

Honest when it doesn't know

If a listing doesn't say, SAL says unknown. It never guesses.
